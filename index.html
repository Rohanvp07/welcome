Ritika,

You're about to trade Mumbai humidity for Coventry drizzle, and honestly, the drizzle doesn't stand a chance against you. University of Warwick has no idea what's coming.

I know moving countries for a course is a big, slightly terrifying, mostly exciting thing. New city, new timetable, new understanding of what "cold" means. But you've got a whole welcome party of one very over-invested friend waiting on this side, plus a list of places that will make Coventry feel like yours faster than you'd think.

Coventry, decoded

A few things that'll make the first weeks easier.

🚉

Coventry station to Warwick campus

The X17/U1 buses run regularly from the city centre to Warwick's campus, and it's about 20–25 minutes. Get an app-based bus pass early — it's cheaper than paying per ride.

🛍️

Groceries and Indian essentials

Foleshill Road has proper Indian grocery stores for spices, snacks, and things Tesco will never stock. Worth the trip in the first week.

🌧️

The weather will lie to you

Sunny mornings turn into rain by lunch, often. Keep a compact umbrella in your bag permanently — not just when it "looks like rain."

🏛️

Coventry itself is worth exploring

The Cathedral ruins next to the new Cathedral, Coventry Transport Museum, and the canal basin are good low-key weekend spots when you need a break from campus.

📱

SIM and bank sorted early

Sort a UK SIM and a student bank account in the first few days — most freshers' desks on campus help with both, and it saves a lot of admin stress later.
